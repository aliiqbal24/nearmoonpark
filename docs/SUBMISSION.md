# Moonpark submission preparation

Track: **Games With Real Economies** (`games`). Title: **MOONPARK — A NEAR Future**.

## Description draft

Build the Moon's best day out. Rockets deliver tourists with finite wallets. You build mass-driver coasters, tune tickets and launch speeds, connect paths and support independent lunar concessionaires. Guests arrive and return on recurring shuttles, walk, queue, spend and form opinions; businesses buy stock, adapt prices, deliver supplies and struggle when cash runs short. Back three named merchants, fund their working capital and own transferable NEAR concession certificates. Earn a fixed share of their sales and a reserved Tourism Board grant by delivering a service target. Make a thriving little lunar economy in one nine-minute opening day.

NEAR enforces the economic simulation and calculates the score. A shared Rust engine runs in browser WebAssembly for smooth prediction and inside the contract for authoritative settlement. Sponsored testnet accounts and restricted session keys remove wallet extensions and gas prompts. Signed merchant agreements fix the revenue share, issue NEP-171 ownership and reserve a capped reward from a funded testnet pool. Transfer a concession outside the game; its new owner can collect the unclaimed reward. A separate funded competition escrow allocates payouts from frozen standings. The keyless contract has no upgrade key or general withdrawal. It protects payout reserves from game-storage costs. Inspect actual transfers, ride the mass driver, then compare the top five.

NPC treasuries are contract-managed virtual balances. No independent NPC wallet custody, NEAR Intents, NEP-245 tokens or live AI dialogue is claimed. The conditional creator-funded giveaway is disclosed as unfunded and dependent on a hackathon win; prize eligibility opens only after gameplay review, rules freeze and verified sign-in.

4. Add a drink concession. Follow a real stock purchase, delivery rover, customer sale and revenue-share transfer in the ledger.
5. Increase time to 4×. Show condition/power pressure and a useful response: maintenance or solar capacity.
6. Back Omar: one click creates his working capital and your concession. Show its 20% income, close the day, collect the funded testnet reward and open its actual NEAR payment receipt. Optionally transfer the certificate and show the owner changed onchain.
7. Show the contract-calculated result, signed-in competition, separate funded testnet escrow and precise conditional USD pledge. Explain that its allocation follows frozen standings, with human confirmation of the external award condition.

Keep the demonstration focused on the park and a visible consequence of a decision. Explain the shared-engine proof briefly after showing the game.
