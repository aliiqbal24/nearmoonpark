# Moonpark trailer production and credits

The 45-second trailer opens with a fictional animated meeting between the game's Illia Polosukhin, Mert Mumtaz and Haseeb Qureshi cameos. Its on-screen disclosure identifies the AI scene and synthetic voices. Haseeb and Mert's newly synthesized dialogue uses voice matching from public interview excerpts. The announcer is an original designed cinematic voice. Illia's moon-pie gag plays visually with a park intercom announcement.

Actual gameplay follows the opening. It shows map-drawn coaster design, a sixty-metre hill, appearance choices, construction, rocket arrivals, finite vendor cash and stock, park publication, paid licensing, creator earnings and an independent shared-park visit. The established resort shots use the game's editable attraction models. Editing compresses the gameplay timing.

The captured license costs 0.005 tNEAR and credits 0.0045 tNEAR to its creator. The recorded confirmation and creator balance are genuine testnet exhibition transactions. [Captured license receipt](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/5oSf4MTiYQH1zWLZ1vggiG2hhtMMCK5e2C28cqS3QTJB)

## Tools and source materials

- Original keyframes use the built-in ImageGen tool and the established fictional cameo portraits. Local animation uses Wan2.2 A14B and TI2V 5B, QuantStack GGUF quantizations and LightX2V distillation through ComfyUI. [Wan2.2](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2), [QuantStack models](https://huggingface.co/QuantStack/Wan2.2-I2V-A14B-GGUF), [LightX2V](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/Wan2.2-Distill-Loras), [ComfyUI](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI)
- Local Qwen3-TTS generates the requested voice-matched cameos and original announcer/intercom voices. References come from public Haseeb and Mert interviews. [Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS), [Haseeb reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAdUOvjt0rI), [Mert reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NQnQS_Wu1t8)
- Sound design combines original synthesized stereo effects, Moonpark effects and CC0 metal restraint foley from Kenney Impact Sounds. [Kenney pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
- Typography uses Archivo Black under the SIL Open Font License. The bundled license is in `public/fonts/ArchivoBlack-OFL.txt`.
- FFmpeg, native MLT and the Kdenlive production connector assemble the picture and separate music, dialogue and effects tracks. Original sources, generation graphs, voice settings, editable HTML graphics, frame-based edit JSON and lossless shot intermediates are preserved in the local production workspace.
