# Moonpark presentation and credits

The persistent Three.js park uses original procedural lunar terrain, rides, astronauts, rovers, rockets, kiosks and a NEAR observatory. Actual visitor state drives arrivals, walking, queues, purchases, occupancy and boarding. Supply vehicles represent paid orders. A shuttle descends, docks, unloads and collects homebound visitors every forty simulation seconds. Eight-person inbound groups continue until all 96 guests have arrived. Merchant owners stand beside their businesses and can be selected.

Actual positive ledger receipts produce coin trails and sounds. Partnership completion has a distinct larger reward. Nearby receipts merge at high speed, and the cash HUD counts smoothly. The park and canvas stay mounted across decisions and at closing.

Drag to pan, scroll/pinch or use zoom controls, right-drag to orbit, and use Home to reset. Hover/select visitors or buildings to inspect them. The mass driver has a front-seat camera and a visible vehicle nose. Blueprint previews show validity and the entrance; smart connection avoids occupied land. Mobile controls retain construction and inspection while reducing render resolution/shadow costs.

Earth and stars occupy a separate, distant sky. Camera translation does not create local parallax for Earth, and its small apparent size, shaded surface and slow rotation give it distance. The shaped lunar terrain extends to the horizon. Lowering the orbit camera toward the horizon reveals the sky; the default building view looks down into the park.

The opening is now a rendered 40-second film, with eased camera paths, controlled cuts, a score, locally synthesized narration and original effects. It is skippable, has desktop/mobile versions, dissolves into gameplay and retains a live fallback. Reduced-motion settings use a still presentation. Production sources and verification are in `MOONPARK-CINEMA.md`. Character/ride models retain the prototype's readable low-poly style; further artwork can follow gameplay review.

Earth diffuse texture, bundled locally: https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg . Upstream Three.js project/MIT notices: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js . Interface icons use Lucide. Narration uses Kokoro 82M v1.0 (Apache-2.0), through Kokoro ONNX (MIT); the production document links their primary sources. All camera work, procedural scene geometry, score, effects and narration text are original. No Bullfrog or Supercell footage, music, logos or game assets are used.

The earlier Mars presentation notes remain in `MARS-VISUALS.md`.
