# Simulation v1

One turn is one Martian sol. All quantities are fictional gameplay units; money and material quantities are integers. Prices have no relationship to NEAR's market price.

## Actors and starting state

Power / water / greenhouse / oxygen / workshop: 1,500 credits each. Household groups: 900 / 450 / 180. Public treasury: 2,600. Total credit supply: 11,630, fixed for the run.

Starting stocks: 120 food; 180 market water plus 40 greenhouse water; 96 oxygen; 50 parts. Separate finite emergency cache: 80 food, 120 water, 96 oxygen. Battery: 180. Starting condition: 86%. Population: 120, with 12 surveyors outside the settlement.

Full-condition production: 490 power / 105 water / 58 food / 64 oxygen / 12 parts per sol. Institutional power targets: 80 water / 110 food / 85 oxygen / 105 workshop. Greenhouse water requirement: 18 per full harvest, reduced to 13 with recycling. Water recycling also reduces household water demand 20%.

Normal household demands: floor(population/3) food, floor(population/2) water, floor(2×population/5) oxygen. Groups receive shares 40% / 35% / remainder. These are aggregated household cohorts; individual worker assignment is abstracted into payroll, fatigue, and wellbeing.

Daily payroll: 160 / 70 / 95 / 70 / 80. Wages distribute 40% / 35% / remainder between household groups. Two unpaid payrolls halve that institution's output. Wellbeing and fatigue reduce productive labor. Credits never mint; public spending, wages, purchases, and taxes circulate the original supply.

## Market order

Apply events; wages; solar generation; power purchases by chosen priority; opening-stock greenhouse water purchases; production; public programs; household purchases; maintenance; taxes; price updates; deprivation and narrative records.

Household budgets are split across remaining essential categories, preventing one purchase from consuming the entire budget. Stock entitlement is proportional within each group; integer division rounds down. The public supply pool distributes purchased or released essentials before household market spending. Early emergency support only covers unmet household demand through sol 12, while the cache lasts.

Goods are consumed only through household consumption, production inputs, maintenance, construction, and the finite aid actions. Excess electrical generation expires each sol. Local atmospheric/raw material extraction is abstracted by the water, oxygen, and workshop production recipes, rather than limited by a separately traded ore token.

## Prices and maintenance

Initial prices by good: food 8, water 4, oxygen 6, parts 24, power 1. Floors: 6 / 3 / 4 / 21 / 1. Ceilings: three times the corresponding initial price. The floor is a tuned operating-cost safeguard, not a universal market-clearing theorem.

Below half target stock, raise price up to 15%; above twice nominal daily output, lower up to 15%; otherwise hold. Power uses generation in place of unsold stock. The tooltip exposes stock, target, previous/current price, condition, and payroll arrears.

Four institutions need two workshop parts daily; the workshop uses one. Missing parts reduce condition, which reduces output. Workshop expansion diverts 25% of its output during construction. Surplus corporate balances above 1,800 credits are taxed by one eighth per sol.

## Events and ending

Seed and sol determine the weak-harvest and minor-weather variation. The pump seal fault on sol 12 reduces ice-works condition by 12. Storm sols 19–21 generate 55% solar power. Exogenous events do not depend on the number or order of client requests.

Household shortages lower wellbeing and accumulate deprivation. Sustained critical deprivation after the teaching period can kill residents. Below 90 residents, or after sol 15 with wellbeing below 35, at least six hardship sols, and outstanding deprivation, the colony fails. A failed ending means the colony's survival system has collapsed; remaining population is the last recorded head count, not a claim that everyone died at once.

The strongest ending requires at least 108 initial settlers, wellbeing ≥60, two normal sols of reserves, and a conservative three-sol normal-weather projection without emergency supplies or rationing. The projection must maintain people and wellbeing, cover normal production demand (including industry water), hold reserves, and keep operational institutions.

Seed 42 has 6,309 legal completed decision paths from 6,561 possible option sequences. Other prefixes include unaffordable options; every legal prefix has at least one valid continuation. All four endings are reachable. The precise current counts are regenerated by `cargo run --release -p last-signal-sim --example search`.
